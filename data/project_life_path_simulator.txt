# Project: Life Path Simulator — AI-Powered Life Simulation Game

## Overview
An AI-powered life simulation game where you navigate decisions from age 10 to 80. Every choice updates a live stat system, and a custom-trained transformer model generates consequences based on your full decision history — then explains why that outcome happened.

## Why I Built This
Most AI portfolio projects are wrappers around ChatGPT. I wanted to build something where I owned the entire stack — the data, the model architecture, the training loop, and the product on top of it. I wanted to understand what it actually takes to train a transformer from scratch and integrate it into a real interactive product.

The core idea came from thinking about how small decisions compound over time. Skipping homework at age 12 shouldn't just be a one-off event — it should quietly lower your discipline stat, which affects what opportunities are available at 25. The AI needed to understand causality across a lifetime, not just respond to a single prompt.

## How It Works
- You are presented with life decisions at each age milestone
- You pick one
- The AI generates a consequence based on your current stats and full decision history
- It explains why that outcome happened — referencing patterns in your past choices
- A visual decision tree grows on screen as you play
- Alternate paths branch off in green when you explore what could have been

## The Custom AI Model
This is NOT a wrapper around an existing API. It is a custom GPT-style decoder-only transformer trained from scratch using PyTorch.

Architecture:
- Type: Decoder-only transformer
- Parameters: 88 million
- Layers: 12
- Embedding dimension: 768
- Attention heads: 12
- Vocabulary size: 1,979 tokens
- Training examples: 50,000 synthetic
- Validation loss: 0.235
- Framework: PyTorch

Training data was generated synthetically using a hand-crafted causal rule engine. Each example is a structured (age, stats, decision) → consequence pair. 25% of examples are counterfactual pairs that teach the model to reason about alternate outcomes.

The model receives the full decision history with every call, enabling explanations like: "A recurring pattern of 'skip homework' choices has shaped the conditions that led here. Low discipline — built up across many small choices — made this outcome harder to avoid."

## Tech Stack
- Model: PyTorch — custom transformer architecture
- Tokenizer: Custom word-level tokenizer
- Backend: FastAPI, Uvicorn
- Frontend: React, React Flow
- Training: CPU (MacBook M-series)

## What I Learned
- How to design and train a small transformer from scratch including data pipeline, tokenizer, and training loop
- How to structure synthetic training data for causal reasoning rather than general text generation
- How prompt design affects model output quality — framing as structured prediction rather than open-ended generation made a significant difference
- How to serve a PyTorch model through a REST API and connect it to a React frontend
